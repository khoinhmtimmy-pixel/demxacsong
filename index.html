Đêm Xác Sống

You are stranded in a vast abandoned town at midnight. Kill zombies to earn coins, then buy guns (500 of them) and upgrades in the shop. Find chests on the map and stand near them to open, or buy chests in the shop. Coins, guns and upgrades are saved between runs.

Move: W A S D · Aim and shoot: mouse · Reload: R · Sprint: Shift · Pause: P
Switch gun: Q/E, mouse wheel or keys 1–5 (pick by class) · Shop: B (any time) · Pet skill: F · Items: Z X C V · Quick chat online: T
Phone: touch the left half to move, the right half to aim and shoot. The bottom buttons reload, sprint and pause.
